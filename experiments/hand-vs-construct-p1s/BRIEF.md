# Shared brief: Bambu Lab P1S, two ways

The same model built twice, once as a hand-written Blender script (`hand/`) and once as a Construct
scene (`construct/`). Same facts, same feature list, about 25 minutes each, rendered from one camera.

## Facts (checked 2026-11-08)

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Outer size W x D x H | 389 x 389 x 458 mm | Cartridge World AU, MatterHackers listings |
| Build volume | 256 x 256 x 256 mm | Makerpoint, printer-hub.ru |
| Weight | 12.95 kg, printer alone | Cartridge World AU |

Everything else below is from general knowledge of the machine, not measured: each build marks it as estimated.

## Features both builds must have

1. Enclosure: a box frame with dark corner posts, clear side panels.
2. Front glass door, hinged on its left edge, that opens.
3. Glass top lid, hinged at the back, that opens.
4. Base with the small front screen and buttons, bottom left of the front.
5. Square heated bed, 256 mm, on two Z lead screws at the back corners; lowered a little.
6. CoreXY gantry: two Y rails along the sides at the top, one X rail across, toolhead on it.
7. Spool holder on the back with a spool, and a PTFE tube to the top.
8. Four feet.

Outcome measured: wall time, tokens added to the session, part count, defects found, what it took
to open the door, and the look from one fixed camera.
